**Code of Assessment Rules for Coursework Submission**

Deadlines for the submission of coursework which is to be formally assessed will be published in course documentation, and work which is submitted later than the deadline will be subject to penalty as set out below. The primary grade and secondary band awarded for coursework which is submitted after the published deadline will be calculated as follows:

1. in respect of work submitted not more than five working days after the deadline
   1. the work will be assessed in the usual way;
   2. the primary grade and secondary band so determined will then be reduced by two secondary bands for each working day (or part of a working day) the work was submitted late.
2. work submitted more than five working days after the deadline will be awarded Grade H.

Penalties for late submission of coursework will not be imposed if good cause is established for the late submission. You should submit documents supporting good cause via MyCampus.

**Penalty for non-adherence to Submission Instructions is 2 bands**

**Video Presentation (5%)**

1. **Introduction**

As a team, you will be required to submit a short video presentation about your project. The weighting for this component of the assessment is 5%.

1. **What should be included in the submission**

The video presentation should be a **minimum of 3 minutes** and a **maximum of 6 minutes** in duration, and should include the following:

1. A **description of the design**, using material from your Design Specification;
2. A **demonstration of the web application** that you have built (deployed on a web server, not local host).
3. An **overview of each team member’s contribution** to this part of the coursework (in %) and what they contributed to it.

Your video presentation needs to be an MP4 video file.

You should discuss within your team who is going to present which part(s). It is not necessary for every team member to speak/present.

1. **How to submit**

Your Video Presentation must be saved as an MP4 video file. One member of the team should submit the MP4 file via the “Video Presentation” submission icon on the Moodle page of the course.

# Working in a team

All members of a team will usually receive the same mark. Where members of a team find the contribution of other members to be unsatisfactory, you are encouraged to resolve the issue internally by negotiation first. However, if this is not possible, the course coordinators should be asked to intervene as soon as possible.

The mark awarded to individual members of the team may be adjusted to reflect the individual’s contribution. **We will be using Deltas as a way of adjusting the team mark in order to arrive at an individual’s mark for the team-based components of the course.**

Each member of a team will be required to submit a form, giving their own as well as their team members’ contributions. A Delta typically adjusts the team mark up or down by 0 or more bands for a given individual according to the contribution. The computation of these Deltas will be informed by the percentage scores that each member of the team will provide, which gives a numerical estimate of the proportion of the overall effort undertaken by each person (including themselves). We will also look at the contributions listed in the video to validate any adjustments.
